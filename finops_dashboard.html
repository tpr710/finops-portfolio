Resource Scheduling

Automate Staging Compute Power Cycles

Implement automated instance state boundaries to shut down non-essential Staging EC2 and EKS nodes overnight and over weekends (off ~65% of total weekly calendar hours).

Estimated Savings $76,947 / yr 65% reduction in Env spend
Rightsizing

Scale Down Over-Provisioned Dev Assets

Downsize consistently underutilized compute nodes in the development environments from large configurations to optimized instances based on historical CPU/memory profiling metrics.

Commitment Management

Procure 1-Year Reserved Instances (RIs) for Prod EC2

Lock in baseline predictable production compute layers with a 1-year, No Upfront Reserved Instance contract. Mitigates volatile premium on-demand pricing rates without architecture changes.
